# Art credits

The detailed Petite Pale Free Roam sprites use character geometry, textures and
hair guides from Mikel007's
[Christmas Crip Part 3 - Belly dancer girl](https://blendswap.com/blend/22248),
listed as CC0 on the author's BlendSwap profile. The materials were adapted and
the character was posed, lit and rendered into eight-direction game sprites.

The Avatar's walk uses `Walk_Loop` from Quaternius's
[Universal Animation Library](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html)
(CC0). The animation was retargeted to the Avatar and baked into the
eight-direction walk sprites.

The Avatar's one-handed swing and unarmed jab use the library's `Sword_Attack` and `Punch_Jab` clips.

The Avatar's hit reaction uses the library's `Hit_Chest` clip.

The Avatar's jog, sprint, interaction, casting, jump, seated poses, roll, and defeat animation also use clips from the free Quaternius library.
