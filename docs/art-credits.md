# Art credits

The detailed Petite Pale Free Roam sprites use character geometry, textures and
hair guides from Mikel007's
[Christmas Crip Part 3 - Belly dancer girl](https://blendswap.com/blend/22248),
listed as CC0 on the author's BlendSwap profile. The materials were adapted and
the character was posed, lit and rendered into eight-direction game sprites.
