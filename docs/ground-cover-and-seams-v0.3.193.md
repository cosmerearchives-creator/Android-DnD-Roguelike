## Geometry extraction
Each input GLB contains a single merged mesh, with hundreds of disconnected geometry islands. Blender 5.3 connected-component analysis found 614, 682, 327 and 300 mesh fragments respectively. Using spatial placement and manual grouping from render previews (NOT splitting individual leaves into separate world items), all islands were assigned to complete candidates. The grouped derivative GLBs are `godot/assets/ground_cover/{woodland_bonus,plants,debris,elements}.glb`, with 12, 11, 13 and 12 individually named mesh nodes. All models are re-centered and ground-aligned at local origin, preserving UVs and original materials. For efficiency, each collection embeds its shared original material map(s) only once, rather than multiplying textures across 48 files. Original image bytes inside derivatives exactly match original GLB images by SHA256. Reproducible Blender extraction and detailed per-variant vertex counts / dimensions: `blender/ground_cover/extract_grouped_assets.py`, `blender/ground_cover/variant_audit.json`.

## Game integration
`godot/ground_cover_layer.gd` is connected to `world.gd`. Deterministic random placement by actual six-terrain biome, with seeded rotation/scale, sparse mud and rocks, greater grassy/mossy density, gaps around tavern and open immediate player spawn. Some objects appear near cell edges to partially disguise grid seams; individual objects have no physics collisions. One MultiMesh batch per visible source variant (40); four original shared-texture GLBs loaded in stages, not thousands of scene/node instances. Initial scope is the 530-cell terrain area near the hub/tavern, not the whole enormous temporary forest floor; extend with procedural chunks later.

## Seam follow-up
The v0.3.192 surface shader retains its edge-only 0–30-inch different-biome blend and original source PBR. Added same-material *world-space* texture sampling only within a narrow band near the shared edge, plus vertex-shader flattening of the outer ~6.5% of each irregular imported tile to a common top elevation to reduce thin black sidewall seam shadows. The tile interiors remain unchanged; source GLB vertices and material images are untouched. Tested an overlaid seam-strip experiment, found in screenshot to make grid lines *worse*, and removed it before release. **Visible square seams remain**, and true seamless continuous terrain would require a follow-up baking/chunked-material pass. The new cover is an additional partial visual improvement, not a guarantee of invisible lines.
